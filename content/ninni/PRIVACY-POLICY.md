## In short

- There is no account in Ninni. We have no server and the app does not connect to the internet.
- Everything you enter about yourself or your baby stays on your phone; it is never sent to us.
- The microphone is used only for two features you turn on. Cry detection never records audio.
- We use no ads, tracking or analytics.
- Subscriptions are handled by Apple; your payment details never reach us.

## 1. What Ninni is

Ninni is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that helps babies fall asleep: shushing, white noise and other
soothing sounds, cry detection, an age-based sleep schedule and a night report.

## 2. What is processed and where it stays

### Microphone

The microphone is used only for two optional features you turn on:

- **Cry detection:** Sound is analysed in real time by Apple's on-device sound classifier and discarded
  immediately. No audio is recorded, stored or sent. Only the time a cry was detected is kept on your
  phone for the night report.
- **Record your voice:** A shush or lullaby you record is stored only in Ninni's own folder on your phone.
  You can delete it in the app at any time.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in iPhone **Settings › Ninni**.

### Stored on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never transferred to us or any third party:

- Your baby's name (optional) and date of birth, entered for the sleep schedule
- Sleep and wake records (last 30 days)
- Night reports: how long sound played and when crying was detected (last 14 nights)
- Your recordings, the last sound played and your settings

### Apple Watch, widget and Live Activity

Apple Watch remotely controls Ninni on your iPhone, over Apple's encrypted connection directly between the
two devices. The widget and the Live Activity (Dynamic Island) show the sound playing and the timer; this
can be visible while the phone is locked.

### Notifications

Cry alerts and the sleep-time reminder are local notifications: they are created on your phone and not
sent from a server. Ninni works without notification permission.

### Purchases

Ninni Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store.

- Apple takes the payment; your name, payment details and Apple Account never reach us.
- The app asks Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active.
- You can manage or cancel it in iPhone **Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions**.

### Backups

If iCloud or computer backup is on, the data above may be included in Apple's device backup. That backup
lives only in your account; we cannot access it.

## 3. What we do not collect

- Name, email, phone number or account details
- Audio recordings (other than ones you make yourself, which stay on your phone)
- Location, contacts, photos or camera data
- Advertising identifier or device fingerprint
- Usage statistics or crash reports (apart from anonymous diagnostics Apple shares with developers if
  you allow it)
- Cross-app or cross-site tracking data

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, because we have no access to it.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- Sleep records are deleted automatically after 30 days, night reports after 14 nights.
- You can delete recordings one by one in the app.
- Deleting the app removes all Ninni data from your phone.
- We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete.

## 6. Security

Data is protected by your device's own security (passcode and file encryption).

## 7. Children

Ninni is designed for parents. No personal data is collected from children; what you enter about your
baby never leaves your phone.

## 8. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device under your control, you can exercise access and deletion rights
directly in the app and in iPhone settings. For questions under your local data protection law (such as
KVKK or the GDPR), contact us at info@woowcode.com.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. If a
change affects how your data is processed, we will also let you know in the app.
