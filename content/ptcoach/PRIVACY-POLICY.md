# PT Coach Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 26, 2026
**Developer:** WOOWCODE YAZILIM ve TİCARET A.Ş., Şirinyalı Mah. 1533. Sokak No:49/2, Muratpaşa/Antalya, Türkiye
**Contact:** info@woowcode.com

PT Coach is an iPhone app for personal trainers to manage their clients, sessions and payments, and for clients to follow their own program and progress. PT Coach has no server that holds your data. Everything entered in the app is stored in the trainer's Apple iCloud account and on the users' own devices; WOOWCODE cannot access it.

## 1. Where Your Data Is Stored

- Everything the trainer enters (clients, sessions, packages, payments, measurements, programs, photos, messages) is stored in the private CloudKit database of the trainer's iCloud account.
- When a client joins with a QR code, the trainer shares only that client's record with them through iCloud sharing. Clients see their own information; they cannot see other clients or the trainer's private notes.
- A client's "I can't make it" requests, messages and consents are written to an area in the trainer's iCloud account that is shared only with that client.
- To work offline, the app keeps a copy of the data on the device, protected by iOS data protection (file encryption). Progress photos are excluded from device backups.
- Storage, encryption and data centers are subject to Apple's iCloud terms.

## 2. What Data Is Processed

The trainer enters the following information for their service relationship with the client:

- Identity and contact: first and last name, phone, date of birth, gender, emergency contact
- **Health data (special category):** medications, chronic conditions, injury history, allergies, pre-exercise health screening, body measurements
- **Progress photos:** only with the client's explicit consent in the app
- Session, package and payment records; training programs and nutrition suggestions; messages

The trainer's own information: name, specialties, working hours and the coaching certificate uploaded for verification.

## 3. Trainer Verification

When signing up, the trainer uploads their coaching certificate. The text on the certificate is read **on the device** with Apple's Vision framework and the name on it is compared with the name entered. The image is never sent to a server.

## 4. Client Consent

When joining with a QR code, the client separately confirms in the app that they:

1. Have read the privacy notice,
2. Give explicit consent to the processing of their health data,
3. Optionally, give explicit consent to storing progress photos.

The version and date of each consent are kept in the trainer's records.

## 5. Who Can See It

- A client's information can be seen only by **their trainer** and **the client**.
- "Trainer Notes" are visible to the trainer only.
- Notifications never show health data.
- WOOWCODE cannot access, view or process this data.

## 6. Data We Collect

- PT Coach contains no advertising, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs; we do not collect usage statistics.
- If "Share With App Developers" is turned on on your iPhone, Apple may send us crash and performance reports that do not identify you.
- If you email us, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

## 7. Subscription and Payments

- The trainer subscription (PT Coach Pro) is purchased through the App Store and processed by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.
- Payments clients make to their trainer are only **recorded** in the app; PT Coach does not collect any money.

## 8. Deletion and Your Rights

- **Trainers** can permanently delete all PT Coach data in iCloud and on the device from Settings → "Delete account and all data". To cancel the subscription, use the App Store subscription settings.
- When a **trainer** deletes a client, all of that client's records (packages, payments, sessions, measurements, photos, messages) are deleted from iCloud and the device.
- **Clients** can end their access and send their trainer a deletion request from Settings → "Leave my trainer and request deletion of my data".
- The trainer, who decides why and how client data is processed, is the data controller for that data. You can exercise your rights under Turkish data protection law (KVKK) with your trainer first.

## 9. International Transfers

iCloud data may be stored in Apple data centers outside Türkiye.

## 10. Children

PT Coach is not designed for people under 18.

## 11. Changes

When we update this policy, we change the effective date and publish it on this page.
