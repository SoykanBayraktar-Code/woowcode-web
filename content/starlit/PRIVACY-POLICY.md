## In short

- You don't create an account in Starlit. We don't run a server.
- What you write and log stays on your phone. If you like, you can turn on iCloud backup and back it
  up to the private iCloud area of your own Apple Account. We can't see it, on your phone or in iCloud.
- We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.
- Your health data is only read; it is not stored, not written to iCloud and not sent anywhere.
- AI suggestions are generated on your phone; what you write is not sent to a server.
- When you delete the app, all Starlit data on your phone is deleted; you can delete the iCloud
  copy from your iPhone's iCloud settings.

## 1. What Starlit is

Starlit is a self-help app inspired by cognitive behavioral therapy techniques. It is not a
medical device; it does not diagnose and is not a substitute for treatment. In an emergency, call
the emergency number where you are.

## 2. What data is processed and where it lives

### Your records

The following are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud backup, they are also backed up to
your own iCloud area as described under "iCloud backup" below. None of them is transferred to us or
to any third party:

- Your routines, completion records and to-do list

- Mood entries, emotion tags, energy and sleep quality ratings, and the thought line you add to a mood entry
- Notes, thought records and parked thoughts
- Ratings of how you felt after a routine, and your predictions
- Letters you write to yourself and your "good moment" entries
- Your answers to the two-week check-in
- Your state in the 12-Week Journey: which stop you are at, the dates the stops opened, and the end-of-phase summaries
- Your hard-day plan: your early signs, what helps you, the first small thing you'll do and, if you choose to write it, the "someone I can call" line (Starlit does not access your contacts)
- Weekly star letters (short summaries written on your device from your own records, using fixed templates)
- "I stepped into daylight" and "I sent someone light" entries (the date only; who you reached out to is not stored)

### iCloud backup (optional)

iCloud backup starts turned off; you can turn it on in Starlit's settings.

- While it is on, the records above are stored in the private iCloud area of your Apple Account
  (the CloudKit private database) and synced across your devices signed in with the same Apple
  Account. That way your records come back when you change phones.
- This area is subject to Apple's iCloud terms. Only you can access it; we can't.
- Your health data and the observations drawn from it are not written to iCloud. Your approximate
  location also stays only on your device.
- When you turn the backup off, the records on your phone stay where they are. You can delete the
  iCloud copy on iPhone by tapping your name at the top of Settings, then iCloud › Manage Storage ›
  Starlit.

### Health data (Apple Health)

If you allow it, Starlit **only reads** the following from Apple Health: step count, sleep,
resting heart rate, mindful minutes and time in daylight.

- This data is held in memory while the app is open, to show you observations of your own.
  Starlit does not save it to its own storage.
- Your health data is not written to iCloud, is not used for advertising or marketing, and is not
  shared with third parties.
- Starlit does not write data to Apple Health.
- You can turn the permission off at any time under Settings › Health › Data Access, or in
  Starlit's own settings.

### Location

If you allow it, Starlit uses your approximate location to work out sunrise and sunset times
where you are.

- The location is rounded to a precision of about 10 kilometers and stored only on your device.
- The times are calculated on your device, without an internet connection. Your location is not
  sent anywhere.
- No location history is kept; only the most recent approximate location is stored.
- If you don't give permission, the app works the same way with default times.

### Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled on your phone and are not sent from a
server. The name of your routine may appear in the notification text.

### Home Screen widget and Lock Screen timer

The widget shares a small summary with the app on the same device: your light score and the names
of today's routines. Mood entries, notes and thought records are not shared with the widget.

When you start focus mode, the routine's name and the time left appear on the Lock Screen and in
the Dynamic Island. This can be seen while your phone is locked; it goes away when focus ends.

### Siri and Shortcuts

You can run some of Starlit's actions (logging your mood, completing a routine, parking a
thought, starting focus) with Siri or Shortcuts. In these actions too, Starlit writes your data
only to your device. What you say to Siri by voice may be processed by Apple under its own
privacy terms so that it can handle your request.

### AI routine suggestions

In Guide, small routines are suggested based on your answers to "What feels hard right now?".

- Suggestions are generated on your phone by Apple's on-device AI model (Apple Intelligence). For
  this, the situation you pick, your answers, the short note you may write, your focus areas and the
  names and times of your existing routines are given to the model at that moment only.
- This information is not sent to any server and is not used for anything other than generating
  suggestions. A suggestion you don't add is not saved; one you add is stored like your other routines.
- If your device doesn't have Apple Intelligence, suggestions are picked from a ready-made list in the app.
- If you write something that signals a crisis, the model is not used; support and emergency lines
  are shown instead.

### Voice dictation

In text fields you can speak instead of typing, if you like. For this, Starlit asks for
permission to use the microphone and speech recognition.

- Your voice is turned into text on your phone, by Apple's on-device speech recognition.
- The audio is not recorded, not stored and not sent anywhere.
- Only the resulting text remains; like your other records, it stays on your device.
- If you don't give permission, you keep typing with the keyboard. You can turn the permission
  off at any time in the Settings app.

### App lock with Face ID

If you like, you can lock Starlit with Face ID. The lock is off unless you turn it on.

- The authentication is done by iOS. Starlit does not see, receive or store your face data; it
  only learns whether the authentication succeeded.
- You can turn the lock off at any time.

### Exporting your data

If you like, you can export your records as a file.

- You create the file, and you choose where it is saved and who it is shared with.
- Starlit does not send the file anywhere on its own; the file does not reach us.
- The app or service you share the file with handles it under its own privacy terms. Because the
  file contains your records, keep it somewhere you trust.

### Purchases

Starlit Plus is a subscription sold through the App Store. The payment is handled by Apple; we do
not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store,
on your device, whether a subscription is active. Apple shares sales totals with developers that
do not identify you.

### Preferences

Your preferences, such as sound, reminders and ritual times, and the focus areas you choose during
the introduction (for example "I can't focus") are stored on your device. These choices are used
only to set the app up for you and are not sent anywhere.

## 3. What we don't collect

- Name, email address, phone number or account information
- Advertising identifier or device fingerprint
- Usage statistics or crash reports (other than the anonymous diagnostic data that Apple shares
  with developers, subject to your permission)
- Data that tracks you across apps and websites

## 4. Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data with third parties, because we have no access to your
data.

What you choose to share yourself is the exception: when you open the share sheet in "Send
someone light," the app you pick (Messages, for example) handles the content you send under its
own privacy terms.

## 5. Backups

Starlit's own iCloud backup is described in section 2. Separately, if iCloud Backup or backup to a
computer is turned on for your phone, Apple may include app data in the device backup. These backups are subject to Apple's terms and are not under Starlit's
control.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- Your records stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app.
- You can delete notes, mood entries, thought records, letters, good moments and routines one by
  one inside the app.
- When you delete the app, all Starlit data on your device is deleted.
- If you turned on iCloud backup, the iCloud copy stays when you delete the app; you can delete it
  on iPhone by tapping your name at the top of Settings, then iCloud › Manage Storage › Starlit.
- Because we hold none of your data, you don't need to send us a deletion request.
- Ending a Starlit Plus subscription does not delete anything. Records you made with Plus stay
  on your device; some of them are hidden until Plus is active again.

## 7. Security

Data is stored with the protection your device provides (device passcode and file encryption).
Setting a passcode on your phone is the most important step in protecting your records.

## 8. Children

Starlit is not designed for people under the age of 13 and does not
knowingly collect data from them.

## 9. Your rights

Because your personal data is on your device and under your control, you can exercise your
rights of access, correction and deletion directly inside the app. For questions under the data
protection law where you live (for example GDPR or KVKK), you can contact us: info@woowcode.com

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version on this page and update the effective date.
For a significant change that affects how your data is processed, we also tell you inside the
app.
