## In short

- Portföyüm is an app where real estate agents share their listings with fellow agents.
- You sign in with Apple; there is no separate password.
- Your profile and listings are stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit).
- A listing's district and approximate location are visible to all members. Exact location and
  contact details are kept in protected records and shown only to Pro members.
- Sharing your location with colleagues is optional and can be turned off at any time.
- We don't show ads, we don't track you and we don't sell your data.

## 1. What Portföyüm is

Portföyüm is a listing-sharing network for real estate agents only. Listings carry no price.
The app is not directed to anyone under 18.

## 2. What data is processed and where it lives

### Account

When you sign in with Apple, Apple gives you a user identifier unique to this app. We store it
only as a hash. If Apple shares your name and email address, they are used to create your profile.

### Agent profile

Your name, office name, license number, phone, email address and an optional bio. Your name and
office are visible to other members. Phone and email are kept in a protected record and shown
only to Pro members.

### Listings

Photos, title, description, floor area, rooms, floor, building age, heating, mortgage
eligibility, features, city, district, neighbourhood, address and location. Photos are resized
on your device before upload. City, district and approximate location (about 1 km) are visible
to all members. Neighbourhood, address and exact location are kept in a protected record and
shown only to Pro members.

### Location

Your location is used on your device to show nearby listings on the map and is not sent to us
for that purpose. If you turn on "Konumumu Paylaş" (Share My Location, Pro) in Settings, your
location is stored in a protected record and shown to Pro members on the map. Locations older
than 2 hours are hidden. You can turn sharing off at any time; it turns off automatically when
your Pro membership ends.

### Field Mode

While Field Mode is on, the nearest listing is shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic
Island. This information is produced on your device.

### Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments; we have no access to your card or payment details. To
confirm your Pro membership, the transaction signed by Apple is sent to our privacy server. The
server uses it only for that check and does not store it.

### Kept on your device

Your likes, saves and follows are currently stored only on your device.

## 3. Privacy server

To pass protected records such as exact locations and contact details to Pro members, we use a
server running on Cloudflare. On every request it confirms Pro membership and passes on the
requested records; it does not keep copies of them. Short-lived technical logs (for example the
time of a request) may be kept for security.

## 4. What we don't collect

- We don't collect advertising identifiers or show ads.
- We don't track you across other apps and websites.
- We don't use analytics or usage-tracking tools.
- We don't see your payment details.

## 5. Sharing and transfers

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing. These service providers are used
to run the service: Apple (iCloud, Sign in with Apple, App Store payments) and Cloudflare
(privacy server). Their servers may be located outside Türkiye; your data is protected by their
security measures.

## 6. Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete your listings in the app at any time. You can delete your account in the
app under the Portföyüm tab, Settings, "Hesabı Sil" (Delete Account). Deleting your account
permanently removes your profile, all your listings and their photos, and your protected records
(contact details, exact locations and shared location). If you can't access the app, send a
deletion request to info@woowcode.com; we complete it within 30 days. Deleting your account does
not cancel a Pro subscription; cancel it in your App Store account settings.

## 7. Security

Data is stored on Apple's iCloud infrastructure and sent over encrypted connections. Only you
and our privacy server can read your protected records.

## 8. Children

Portföyüm is intended for real estate professionals and is not directed to anyone under 18.

## 9. Your rights

Under Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where it applies, the GDPR, you have the right to access
your data and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or to object to its processing. Send
requests to info@woowcode.com.

## 10. Changes

If we update this policy, the effective date changes and we announce significant changes in the app.
